# Shenzhen City — Art Bible

## Palette
- **Sky**: Deep navy (#05070d) to dark blue-black
- **Primary accent**: Teal (#14b8a6) — brand color for Shenron
- **Warm lights**: Amber (#f59e0b), soft white (#fef3c7)
- **Cool lights**: Cyan (#06b6d4), blue (#3b82f6)
- **Building facades**: Dark grey (#1e293b), charcoal (#334155)
- **Road**: Dark asphalt (#1a1a2e) with slight blue tint
- **Sidewalk**: Concrete grey (#475569)
- **Vegetation**: Deep green (#166534) to forest (#15803d)
- **Neon signs**: Hot pink (#ec4899), electric blue (#818cf8), lime (#84cc16)

## Lighting
- Moonlight: Cool blue-white directional, low intensity
- Street lamps: Warm amber point lights, 15m radius
- Neon: Colored emissive materials, bloom-glow
- Interior: Warm white, higher intensity
- Fog: Horizon fog starting at 145m, full at 480m

## Materials
- All materials use MeshStandardMaterial or MeshPhysicalMaterial
- Night-time roughness: 0.6-0.9 (not mirror-perfect)
- Metalness: 0.0-0.3 for buildings, 0.8-1.0 for vehicles/signage
- Emissive for neon signs, window lights, dashboard displays

## Scale Reference
- Player height: 1.8m
- Door height: 2.1m
- Storey height: 3.5m
- Street width: 12m (2 lanes + sidewalks)
- Sidewalk width: 3m
- Boulevard width: 24m (4 lanes + median + sidewalks)
