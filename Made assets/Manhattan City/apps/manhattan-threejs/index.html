Everything else
All meshes, materials, facades, vehicles, street furniture, interiors, the HQ tower and its Floor 45, and all audio are generated procedurally in Blender and at runtime from the data above. No purchased kit, no scanned geometry, no photographic texture, no ripped or extracted game assets, and no imagery from any commercial map provider. Buildings are unbranded and vehicles carry no marque.
Current honest state: a city-scale massing model with procedural facades, street life and interiors. It is not photoreal and it is not a finished game.
