# 2O-B — LOD datum fix: every L2/L3/L4 roof sat 12 m below the L0 world

Phase 2O-B. Branch `ds/2o-b-lod` @ c78b7ad (base), fixed and re-measured.

## Root cause

`scripts/phase2/56_build_lods.py` extruded the L2/L3/L4 massing from
`min_h .. h` (raw registry heights). The full-detail world extrudes on the
land plane:

- `scripts/blender_common.py:50` — `LAND_LEVEL = 12.0` (Phase 1 raised the
  land plane to 12 m to cure depth-buffer banding; see the comment there)
- `scripts/20_buildings.py:45` — `GROUND = bc.LAND_LEVEL`
- `scripts/20_buildings.py:432-433` — `z0 = GROUND + min_h`, `top = GROUND + h`

So every L0 building occupies `LAND_LEVEL+min_h .. LAND_LEVEL+h` in world
space, while the pre-fix LOD massing occupied `min_h .. h`: **every L2/L3/L4
roof sat a uniform 12 m below the L0 roof it replaces.** The 2O-A harness
(`bench.measureLodL2`, `err = hitY - h`) was calibrated to the buggy build
("roof at registry height h", `bench.js:594-602`) and could not see it:
it reported median 0.000 over the bug and flagged a different, unrelated
sample (bid 20009) as its max 22.9 m.

## The fix

`scripts/phase2/56_build_lods.py`:

- `LAND_LEVEL = bc.LAND_LEVEL` and `z0 = LAND_LEVEL + min_h`,
  `z1 = LAND_LEVEL + h` — the massing now sits on the same datum as the L0
  tiles (verified `z0 < z1` always: the existing `h <= min_h` guard runs
  first, and `Massing.prism` also rejects `z1 <= z0`).
- Regression guard: `drawn + dropped == registry rows` or the build aborts,
  so a new skip rule can never silently drop buildings again.
- The manifest records `land_level_m: 12.0` and `base: "LAND_LEVEL + min_h"`
  so the runtime and any future measurement know the datum.

## Measurement

Method matches the 2O-A baseline (`bench.measureLodL2({tiles:15, perTile:26})`,
raycast down from `registry height + 30 m`, first hit, far 60 m) plus a
corrected convention and an L2-vs-L0 delta:

- **legacy**     `err = hitY - h`                    (2O-A convention)
- **corrected**  `err = hitY - (LAND_LEVEL + h)`     (the real L0 datum)
- **delta**      `err = hitY_L2 - hitY_L0`           (swap movement, same ray
                   on the tile's L2 glb and its L0 full-detail glb)

Runner: `scripts/qa/lod-recheck.mjs` (zero new packages; Vite :5173 strict,
headless Chrome on a free CDP port, unique user-data-dir; kills both in a
`finally`). Before run: `--label before` on the pre-fix tiles. After run:
`--label after` on the rebuilt tiles. Raw: `docs/qa/evidence/2o-b-lod/lod_recheck.json`
(both runs), `docs/qa/evidence/2o-b-lod/lod_recheck_after.json` (after only).

### Before (pre-fix tiles) vs After (corrected build)

Harness (2O-A method, 15 tiles × 26 samples):

| | samples | median | p90 | max | worst |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| before | 583 | 0.000 | 0.0006 | **22.8993** | bid 20009, tile -02_-03 |
| after  | 582 | 12.000 | 12.0006 | 12.0049 | bid 44989, tile +02_+07 |

The 2O-A convention is datum-blind: it assumes roofs at `h`. The buggy
build satisfied that assumption (median 0) and the corrected build now reads
the uniform +12 datum offset — which is the fix being *visible* under the
old convention. The corrected/delta conventions measure the claim directly.

Inline, every candidate tile (44 tiles, 1173 samples, same samples both runs):

| metric | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| legacy median / p90 / max | 0.000 / 0.0006 / 22.8993 | 12.000 / 12.0006 / 34.8993 |
| **corrected** median / p90 / max | **-12.000 / -11.9994 / 10.8993** | **0.000 / 0.0006 / 22.8993** |
| **delta** (L2−L0) median / p90 / max | **-12.0003 / -11.9994 / 1.4531** | **-0.0003 / 0.0006 / 13.4531** |
| delta min | -34.7892 | -22.7892 |

- The corrected median was **-12.000 m before** (every sampled roof 12 m low)
  and is **0.000 m after** — the datum bug, measured directly.
- The delta median was **-12.0003 before** and **-0.0003 after**: the
  L0→L2 swap moved roofs by −12 m before, and by −0.3 mm after (draco
  20-bit position quantization on a 1400 m tile is 1.3 mm — the residual is
  quantization, not placement).
- Per-tile after (corrected |max| over the 44 sampled tiles): 43/44 tiles ≤
  0.0051 m; the single outlier is tile **-02_-03** at 22.8993 — the bid
  20009 sample, explained below. Delta per-tile after: dMax ≤ 1.3 mm on
  41/44 tiles; the negative tail (−4 … −22.8 m) is the L0 world's own
  parapets/rooftop clutter/crowns sitting above the flat L2 massing roof
  (detail the L2 tier legitimately removes) and the two positive outliers
  (+6.05, +13.45) are samples where the L0 ray fell past a degenerate
  footprint to the ground while the L2 ray hit the massing roof.
- The delta extremes pair across runs *exactly* (+1.4531 → +13.4531,
  −34.7892 → −22.7892, both ±12.000): the two builds differ only by the
  datum shift.

### bid 20009 (the 2O-A worst), tile -02_-03, expected roof 15.0 m

Corrected convention (`err = hitY - 15`), cast on the L2 tile and the L0
full-detail tile `manhattan_-02_-03.glb`:

| cast point | L2 before | L0 | delta | L2 after | L0 | delta |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| interior of ring | -12.0001 | 0.000 | -12.000 | **-0.0001** | 0.000 | **0.000** |
| registry point (-1887.89, -3617.03) | +10.8993 | +22.9005 | -12.002 | +22.8993 | +22.9005 | **-0.002** |

The building's roof is now at 15.000 m in its L2 tile — exactly where the
L0 world extrudes it — so **bid 20009 is present at L2 range with -0.0001 m
error (0.000 vs the L0 world)**. The 2O-A sample point still reads 22.9 m in
*both* L2 and L0 (delta −0.002): that point lies 0.53 m outside bid 20009's
own footprint (a 35.7 m² concave strip whose areal centroid falls outside
its boundary — verified point-in-polygon against `source_data/cache/buildings.pkl`),
so the ray passes over neighbour building 14513 (h = 25.9) in the L0 world
too. It was never a LOD defect; the 2O-A convention misread it as one.

## Claim status

**"LOD 0.00 m"** — previously DISPUTED (2O-A: max 22.8993 m over 583
samples, "bid 20009 absent from its L2 tile"). Re-verified against the
corrected build:

- New max corrected error: **22.8993 m** — that is the same point *in the
  L0 world itself* (delta −0.002 m), i.e. a registry-position vs footprint
  artifact, not LOD movement.
- Every sample over a building's own footprint: corrected |err| ≤ 0.0051 m
  (median 0.000); L2-vs-L0 roof delta median −0.0003 m, p90 +0.0006 m —
  the L0→L2/L3 swap no longer moves a single roof.
- Claim status: **CONFIRMED with corrected convention** (median/p90 ≈ 0;
  max over footprint samples ≤ 0.0051 m), with the 22.9 m point documented
  as a measurement artifact present identically in L0. The 2O-A harness
  convention itself is datum-blind and must not be used to judge this claim.
